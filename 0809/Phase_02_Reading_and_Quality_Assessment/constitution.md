# Constitution: Phase 02 — Reading & Quality Assessment

## 1. Principles
### Rule 1: Non-Destructive Source Preservation
AI recovery tools (AI-02A and AI-02B) MUST NOT add outside facts, rewrite for style, or delete content. Unrepairable portions MUST be isolated in `unresolved_segments`.

### Rule 2: Verifiable Page Continuity
Every extracted block MUST maintain traceability to its source page number (`pageMap`).
