# Constitution: Phase 04 — Pedagogical Part Splitting & Structure

## 1. Principles
### Rule 1: No Orphaned Topics or Fragmented Formulas
Every extracted topic ID (`T-`) MUST be assigned to at least one part. Formulas (`F-`) and their explaining text MUST NOT be split across two separate parts.

### Rule 2: Strict Page Range Guardrails
Document page count dictates recommended part count:
- <=10 pages: 1-3 parts
- <=20 pages: 2-6 parts
- <=30 pages: 4-8 parts
- <=50 pages: 5-10 parts
- <=100 pages: 8-15 parts
If proposed count deviates, an out-of-range warning MUST be emitted.
