# Specification: Phase 08 — Multi-Exam PYQ Intelligence Layer

## 1. Requirements
- **FR-801**: Screen 1 Exam Switch: MPPSC (Prelims/Mains), MP State Exams (SI/Patwari/Vyapam), UPSC CSE, State PSCs (UPPSC, BPSC, RAS, MPSC).
- **FR-802**: Topic-level PYQ extraction matching the topic against known question databases and syllabus rubrics.
- **FR-803**: Model Answer breakdown for 3-markers, 5-markers, and 11-markers (MPPSC) or 10-markers and 15-markers (UPSC).
- **FR-804**: "Examiner Trap" callout warning candidate against confusing terminology and common elimination tricks.

## 2. Gap Traceability
- Eliminates GAP-26 (Absence of targeted competitive exam PYQ analysis).
