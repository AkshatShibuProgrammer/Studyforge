# Constitution: Phase 08 — Multi-Exam PYQ Intelligence Layer

## 1. Principles
### Rule 1: Authentic Exam Attribution
Every previous year question item MUST state the specific exam and year (e.g. `[MPPSC Mains 2021 Paper-II]`, `[UPSC CSE 2022 GS-3]`). Fabricated or unsourced exam labels are forbidden.

### Rule 2: Structured Solution Architecture
Model answers MUST follow competitive exam evaluation standards:
1. Introduction (Crisp definition or context: 1-2 lines)
2. Core Analytical Body (Numbered arguments, legal/constitutional articles, formulas, diagrams)
3. Conclusion / Forward-looking Way Forward (1-2 lines)
